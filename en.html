About the Space

A space made for
your most precious moments

ON THE TOP sits in a high-rise tower between Gwanggyo Lake Park and Gwanggyo Jungang Station, where floor-to-ceiling windows open onto sweeping lake and city-skyline views.

Whether it is your baby's first birthday or 100-day celebration, a bridal shower with close friends, an unforgettable proposal, or a milestone birthday for your parents — every occasion worth remembering becomes a beautiful photograph here.

A private, exclusive space with a thoughtfully styled setting and high-rise lake views — everything from preparation to photography, all in one place.

FAQ

Frequently Asked

How do I make a booking?+

We recommend NAVER online booking (the green buttons at the top and bottom-right of the page), where you can view and pick available slots in real time. You can also message us on KakaoTalk to book.

Is parking available? Is it free?+

Yes — free parking is provided based on your booking duration. For navigation, search "스튜디오 온더탑" (Studio On The Top) in KakaoNavi or TMap.

How do I get there by subway?+

Take the Shinbundang Line to Gwanggyo Jungang Station; it is about a 7-minute walk from there. The space sits between Gwanggyo Lake Park and the station — see the route video below for details.

What kinds of events can I host?+

First birthdays and 100-day celebrations, bridal showers, proposals, milestone birthdays (60th/70th/80th), small meetings and workshops, and small weddings. Feel free to ask about specific setups on KakaoTalk.

The booking page is in Korean — what should I do?+

The NAVER booking page is currently in Korean. If that is inconvenient, just message us on KakaoTalk open chat (English is welcome) and we will help you complete your booking.
